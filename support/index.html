Delete your account (in the app)

You can delete your account yourself inside the Golden Scissors app:

1. Open the app and tap the menu tab (the three-line icon, bottom left).

2. Your account page opens (sign in first if asked).

3. Scroll down and tap "Delete my account", then confirm.

This deletes your account profile and login. Order and invoice records may be kept for a limited period where the law or our accounting records require it, as described in our Privacy Policy. If you cannot sign in, contact us using the details above and we will delete the account for you.
